I work on the infrastructure of knowledge — the standards and structures that make information trustworthy.

Over two decades I've done this across a lot of domains: clinical decision support, precision medicine, biodiversity monitoring, digital twins, open science. The domain changes; the core problem stays the same — how do you organize heterogeneous information so that different communities can share it, trust it, and build on it over time?

My background is in classification and knowledge organization — trained as an information scientist, ended up in research because it kept putting me at the hard edges of that problem. The places where the vocabulary doesn't yet exist, where the data doesn't fit together, where someone has to decide what something means before any of the systems can work.

If you're working on any of that — in science, publishing, cultural heritage, environmental data, open knowledge infrastructure — I'd like to hear what you're doing.
